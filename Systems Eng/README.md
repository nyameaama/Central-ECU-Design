# Systems Eng

These are my working numbers for the first thruster build. I am keeping the engine side lean and leaving tank isolation, pressurization, regulation, venting, filling, draining, and bulk filtration on the tank side.

The engine-side hardware is six normally closed valves, eleven AST20PT points, five thermocouples, the spark igniter, and the Central ECU. The PCB has seven valve channels, with six assigned and one spare.
